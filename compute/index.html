Assumptions and where they come from

Every speed and price is editable. measured values come from completed jobs; derived ones are computed from measured jobs with a stated assumption; budget values are the assumptions of our budget report; published values come from vendor specifications or public benchmarks; a rule of thumb is a judgement call.

Speeds are per GPU and assume the jobs are written for batched inference (vLLM) and standard fine-tuning libraries. Times scale linearly with tokens, which is the main simplification. October 2026.
